## Promotion / dependency policy

- **A first:** inspect and pilot B1 from existing documents. **B later:** safe decision/state proposals only after demand and explicit approval. **C:** only if milestone evidence supports an added contract. **D/E/F/G:** not bundled into the first development slice.
- A reproducible correctness, safety or public-contract bug merits focused regression. Non-blocking friction ordinarily requires at least **3 distinct real tasks**; a blocker with no reasonable workaround at least **2 distinct tasks**; measured, controlled outcomes may support a specific improvement. These are the retained [Phase 7C rules](PHASE_7C_EVIDENCE.md#promotion-rules). Never cite the ten historical Phase 7C tasks as newly collected validation.
- **Prototype exception:** a pre-threshold reversible investigation requires an explicit, documented user decision and cannot automatically graduate to stable capability.
- Capabilities may be **Existing, Partial, Missing, Deferred**, and an implementation candidate can be **unapproved** at any of these statuses. Conflicting descriptions are reconciled against the exact source/commit; not silently overwritten.
- Every separately approved slice records problem, sample evidence, reuse feasibility, scope/non-goals, impact on frozen public v1, implementation/test strategy, exact DoD, rollback, and exit. Stop and defer rather than repeatedly expanding scope.
